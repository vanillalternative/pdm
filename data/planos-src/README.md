# planos-src — sources for the bundled special-instruments registry

`../planos.json` is generated by `gen-planos.mjs` from the three research
inventories in this directory (albufeiras, orla costeira/estuários, áreas
protegidas). The inventories were compiled from the official APA, ICNF,
DGT/PCGT and Diário da República registries and adversarially verified in
July 2026; the generator applies the verification corrections, classifies each
instrument's state, derives token-sequence subject match-keys (used to confirm
"the queried location falls inside this instrument's area" from live layer
attributes), and folds municipality names to CAOP spellings.

Regenerate after editing any source:

    node gen-planos.mjs

Statuses are a snapshot — programas especiais approvals and prorrogações keep
landing (a wave of despachos in 2024–2026). Re-verify against
pcgt.dgterritorio.gov.pt and dre.pt before relying on a status.
